Privacy Policy

Data controller: WAVER STORE (TIN: —), Tyumen, Russia.

This Privacy Policy applies to all information the waverstore.site website (the "Site") may collect about you while using it, and is written in line with Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

1. What data we collect

When placing an order through the website form you may provide: name, phone number, email, city and delivery address or pickup point, and an order comment. This information is provided voluntarily and used only to process your request.

When you simply browse the Site, technical data is collected automatically: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and cookies — via the Yandex.Metrika counter.

2. Why we use this data

  • Processing and confirming your order, contacting you via messenger, phone or email
  • Arranging delivery
  • Improving the Site and analyzing traffic (anonymized Yandex.Metrika statistics)
  • Responding to enquiries via Telegram, WhatsApp, phone or the website form

We do not use your data for purposes other than those listed above and do not sell it to third parties.

3. Who has access to the data

The order form is processed by Formspree, Inc. (USA) — order data is technically routed there for delivery to the Seller's inbox. The address map and suggestions in the order form run on the Yandex Maps API. Visit statistics are collected by Yandex.Metrika. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies (including through Yandex.Metrika) to recognize your browser and analyze traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings — some features of the Site may not work correctly as a result.

5. Retention period

Order data is retained for as long as needed to fulfil the order, plus a reasonable period afterwards to handle any warranty or return questions. Anonymized visit statistics are retained per Yandex.Metrika's own policy.

6. Your rights

You may at any time ask what data we hold about you, request that it be corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent to processing — message us in the seller chat on the marketplace (Ozon, Wildberries, Yandex Market).

7. Consent to processing

By submitting the order form or any other form on the Site, you confirm that you have read this Policy and consent to the processing of the personal data you provide for the purposes listed in Section 2.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this page — the current version is always available at waverstore.site/english/privacy/. Continuing to use the Site after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

9. Contact

For questions about personal data processing: seller chat on the marketplace (Ozon, Wildberries, Yandex Market).
